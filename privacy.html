GuardianX Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

1. Information We Collect

GuardianX does not store or save any personal information. While the bot is running, it may temporarily process the following data only to perform commands:

- Discord usernames
- User IDs
- Server IDs and channel IDs
- Message content used for commands

2. How We Use Information

GuardianX only uses information to execute commands, manage permissions, provide premium features, and maintain bot functionality. No information is shared or sold.

3. Data Storage and Retention

GuardianX does not save any data. All information is processed in real time and cleared when the bot restarts or shuts down.

4. User Rights

Since GuardianX does not store data, users do not need to request deletion. No logs or history are kept.

5. Third-Party Services

GuardianX does not use any third-party tracking or analytics services.

6. Children’s Privacy

GuardianX does not knowingly collect or store information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if new features are added.
